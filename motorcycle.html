about the motorcycle
1958 Harley Davidson Duo-Glide

Standing apart from all of her other creations, Shannon Castle's to-scale reproduction of a Harley Davidson 1958 Duo-Glide Motorcycle is the artist's undeniable masterpiece. This hand-carved sculpture, weighing over 2,000lbs is 9 inches thick, 78 inches long, and 48 inches in height. Unprecedented in art history, the first of it's kind, this creation is a testament to both the skill and tenacity of the artist. Even the blueprints had to be drawn by hand, to scale, in order to begin to execute this design.
